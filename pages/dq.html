Iron Mountain currently isn't able to see patients on Medicaid — this is a government regulation that applies to the practice, not a reflection of your coverage. Your best next step is to check your Medicaid plan's provider directory for an in-network orthopaedic surgeon, or ask your primary care doctor for a referral.

When you're choosing one, here's what's worth holding out for:

  • A surgeon who sees you personally,
    not just a PA.
  • One who starts with the least-invasive option and doesn't rush you toward surgery.
  • One who takes the time to explain what's actually going on and lays out all your options. That's the standard of care you deserve, wherever you end up.

If your coverage changes down the road, we'd be glad to see you

Iron Mountain doesn't currently accept HMO plans directly. That doesn't mean there's nothing we can do — call the office at (215) 821-7059 and the team can walk you through your options.
