What he handles

Shoulder

Knee

Hip
Labral tears, impingement, and the nagging pain that's quietly cut your distance.
Ankle

Wrist

Elbow
Tennis elbow, golfer's elbow, ligament injuries, and the ache that won't let you lift like you used to.
Broken Bones & Trauma
Fractures and injuries that need a surgeon who does this all the time.

Not sure which bucket you're in? That's fine. Tell us what hurts and we'll point you the right way.

The doctor you'll
actually see

Dr. Mark Ayzenberg is a board-certified, fellowship-trained orthopaedic surgeon (MD, FAAOS) in the Philadelphia suburbs, specializing in sports medicine and minimally invasive shoulder, knee, and hip surgery.

He completed his fellowship at the Southern California Orthopaedic Institute and his residency at Einstein in Philadelphia, and he's affiliated with Holy Redeemer Hospital.

He built Iron Mountain Orthopaedic Institute around a simple idea, and it's the line he actually leads with: "I don't treat pictures, I treat patients." Less pills, less shots, less surgery, whenever possible. He'll give you the time the problem deserves, and he'll be straight with you about what surgery can and can't do.

frequently asked
questions

Do I really need surgery?
Maybe not, and that's the point of the visit. Dr. Ayzenberg starts with the least-invasive option that can work. If surgery isn't your best path, he'll tell you honestly and give you a real plan instead.Maybe not, and that's the point of the visit. We start with the least invasive option that can work, and plenty of people leave here without an operation. If surgery isn't your best path, you'll be told honestly and you'll leave with a real plan instead.
Can I see Dr. Ayzenberg himself?
Yes. Tell the office on your scheduling call and they'll book you with him. He does book out, so if you'd rather be seen sooner, Jamie Hermance, his physician assistant, can usually get you in faster. She works side by side with Dr. Ayzenberg, he reviews the plan either way, and if you need surgery he's the one who performs it. Your call, and nobody will rush you into either one.
What does it cost?
A visit here is billed to your insurance the same as any specialist appointment, so your cost is whatever your plan's copay or deductible works out to. Handling it yourself? You'll get the visit cost on your scheduling call, before anything is booked.
Do you take my insurance?
Dr. Ayzenberg works with most major PPO plans. The office will confirm exactly how your plan works when they call, so there are no surprises.Dr. Ayzenberg works with most major PPO plans. When the office calls, they'll confirm exactly how your specific plan works, so nothing surprises you later.
How fast can I be seen?
Answer the questions, then pick a time for a 15-minute call with the office. They'll book your visit on that call, usually within a couple of weeks, sooner if it's urgent.
What if surgery can't help me?
Then he'll say so, and point you toward what actually can. An honest "no" is part of what the visit is for.
What if I do need surgery?
Then Dr. Ayzenberg does it himself, minimally invasive wherever possible. He handles the complicated cases other surgeons pass on, including revisions and reconstructions, and one patient flew six thousand miles for a rare shoulder repair. You'll know exactly what's involved before you agree to anything.
